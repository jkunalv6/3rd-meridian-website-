Relay

Want a smart engineering assistant? Add Relay to your ChatGPT or Claude!

Relay is an add-on that helps your AI understand engineering work. It can follow changes across your files and communications, handle routine coordination and documentation, and help you improve designs using cited guidance and relevant examples from DFMA, manufacturing, materials, testing, and more.

WITHOUT RELAY
