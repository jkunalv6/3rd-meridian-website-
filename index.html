DATA & TRUST

Know what Relay can access before you connect it.

What Relay accesses

Relay uses the prompts and sources you explicitly make available, such as selected files, design context, email, or documents. The exact permissions depend on the client and connectors you configure.

Where information goes

With ChatGPT or Claude, relevant prompts and retrieved context may be processed by that provider under your account and its terms. A private deployment can use an agreed model and hosting environment instead.

What is retained

Relay is designed to retrieve from source systems rather than replace them. Any required coordination state, logs, or memory must be defined for the deployment. Do not assume zero retention unless your configuration and provider terms say so.

Access and deletion

Revoke connected-source access in the relevant client or source system. Provider-held conversations or account data must be managed with that provider. For 3rd Meridian-managed deployments or enquiries, contact us to request access or deletion.

This is a product-level summary, not a certification or a substitute for the terms of your AI, model, hosting, or source-system providers.
